# 🏀 NBA Tutorial for Eunice

Welcome! This guide will take you from **zero NBA knowledge** to being able to hold a great basketball conversation — step by step.

Don't try to read everything at once. Go through each file in order, one day at a time, and you'll be ready to mingle like a pro. 😄

## 💡 Tips Before You Start

- **Don't memorize everything** — just get familiar with the big names and 2–3 fun facts each.
- **Pick a team to root for** — it makes watching so much more fun.
- **Current events matter most** — people talk about *what's happening now*, so Step 2 is the most useful for mingling.
- **YouTube is your friend** — 10-minute highlight videos teach more than any article.

> *"Basketball is not just a sport, it's a culture."* — Kobe Bryant
