# 📖 Step 1: NBA Basics — The Skeleton

> Before memorizing names, understand the game. This takes 20 minutes and unlocks *everything* else.

---

## 🏟️ What Is the NBA?

The **NBA (National Basketball Association)** is the premier professional basketball league in the world, based in the United States and Canada. Founded in **1946**, it has **30 teams** and is the highest level of basketball on Earth.

---

## 🎯 How the Game Works

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Players per side** | 5 on the court at a time (rosters have ~15 players) |
| **Game length** | 4 quarters × 12 minutes = 48 minutes total |
| **Scoring** | 2 pts inside the arc, **3 pts** beyond the arc, **1 pt** free throw |
| **Overtime** | 5-minute OT period if tied at the end |
| **Shot clock** | Teams must attempt a shot within **24 seconds** |

The team with the most points at the end wins. Simple!

---

## 🗺️ League Structure

The 30 teams are split into **2 conferences**:

### Eastern Conference
> Teams from the Eastern US & Canada (e.g., Boston, New York, Miami, Toronto)

### Western Conference
> Teams from the Western US (e.g., Los Angeles, Golden State, Denver, Oklahoma City)

Each conference has **3 divisions** of 5 teams each — but for mingling, East vs. West is all you need to know.

---

## 📅 The Season

```
October → April     Regular Season (82 games per team)
         ↓
April → June        Playoffs (top 8 teams per conference)
         ↓
         June       NBA Finals (East champion vs. West champion)
         ↓
         🏆         CHAMPION!
```

The **playoffs** are best-of-7 series — first team to win 4 games advances. This is when things get *intense*.

## 🔑 Key Terms Cheat Sheet

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Ring** | A championship ring 💍 — the ultimate prize |
| **MVP** | Most Valuable Player (regular season award) |
| **Finals MVP** | MVP of the NBA Finals series |
| **GOAT** | Greatest Of All Time |
| **Triple-double** | 10+ in three stats (pts, rebounds, assists) in one game |
| **Double-double** | 10+ in two stats |
| **Dunk** | Slam the ball down through the hoop aggressively |
| **Block** | Swat away an opponent's shot |
| **Steal** | Take the ball from the opponent |
| **Turnover** | Losing the ball to the other team |
| **Paint / Post** | The rectangular area near the basket |
| **Point Guard (PG)** | The "quarterback" — runs plays, makes passes |
| **Shooting Guard (SG)** | Typically a scorer |
| **Small Forward (SF)** | Versatile, do-everything player |
| **Power Forward (PF)** | Physical, near the basket |
| **Center (C)** | The tallest player, anchors the team near the basket |
| **Franchise player** | The star a team is built around |
| **Trade deadline** | A date midseason when teams can trade players |
| **Draft** | Annual selection of new players from college/international |
| **Buyout** | A player released from contract, free to sign anywhere |
| **Superteam** | When multiple superstars join the same team |
