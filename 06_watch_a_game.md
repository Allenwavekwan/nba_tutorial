# 🍿 Step 6: Watch a Game Together!

> You've done the reading. Now it's time for the real thing — watching an actual NBA game. This is where everything clicks. And it's way more fun with someone who can explain things live. 😄

---

## 🎯 Before You Watch

You don't need to understand everything right away. Focus on **3 things only**:
1. **Who's the star player** to watch on each team?
2. **What's the score** and which team are you rooting for?
3. **Did anything surprising happen?** (a dunk, a buzzer beater, a foul)

That's it. The rest will come naturally over time.

## 🏟️ Best Games to Watch as a Beginner

### For excitement (high-scoring, fun)
- **Golden State Warriors** games — fast pace, lots of 3-pointers, Steph Curry magic
- **Oklahoma City Thunder** games — young, athletic, SGA doing his thing
- **Denver Nuggets** games — Jokić doing bizarre-but-brilliant things

### For star-watching
- Any **Los Angeles Lakers** game — LeBron + Luka, the ultimate star duo
- Any **San Antonio Spurs** game — watch Wembanyama and think about the future

### For rivalries & intensity
- **Lakers vs. Celtics** — the greatest rivalry in NBA history (any time these two play)
- Any **playoff game** — when it matters, the intensity is 10×

## 🕹️ A Beginner's In-Game Guide

### In the first quarter, look for:
- Who are the star players? Which team looks more energetic?
- Watch the **point guard** — they handle the ball the most and control the offense

### During the game, appreciate:
| Play | What to say |
|------|------------|
| A big dunk | *"Did you see that?! He had no business doing that!"* |
| A deep 3-pointer | *"That was way behind the line — how is that even possible?"* |
| A no-look pass | *"He didn't even look! How did he know his teammate was there?"* |
| A chase-down block | *"He came from nowhere to block that!"* |
| A buzzer beater | 🤯 (no words needed — just scream) |

### During timeouts / halftime:
- Check the **box score** (points, rebounds, assists per player)
- Talk about what's surprised you most
- Predict what will happen in the second half

## 🗣️ Things to Say During the Game

Even if you're not sure what's happening, these are always appropriate:
- *"Wow, he's so fast."*
- *"That was such a smart pass."*
- *"How did that not go in?!"*
- *"He's having a great game tonight."*
- *"They really need to stop giving him open shots."*
